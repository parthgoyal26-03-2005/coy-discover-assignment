# Demo portrait artwork

All 36 SVG portraits were created specifically for this assignment. They depict fictional adults and do not represent real people. No externally hosted photos, third-party image APIs, or personal data are used.

The portraits share an illustration style with individual palettes, hairstyles, accessories, and crops. The matching names, biographies, ages, and cities are fictional seed fixtures.

The bundled font packages include their original SIL Open Font License notices in `node_modules/@fontsource`. Lucide icons use the ISC license.
